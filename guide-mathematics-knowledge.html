Mathematics Knowledge

Algebra, expressions, geometry and basic formulas — explanations and worked examples to complement your practice questions.

Solving equations

Use inverse operations to isolate the variable. For 5x + 7 = 42, subtract 7 to get 5x = 35, then divide by 5 to find x = 7. Check by substituting: 5(7) + 7 = 42.

Whatever you do to one side of the equation, do to the other. If a fraction appears, multiplying both sides by a common denominator can simplify the equation.

Order of operations

Work inside parentheses first, then exponents, multiplication/division from left to right, and addition/subtraction from left to right. For 3 + 4 × (8 − 5)², evaluate the parentheses to get 3, square to get 9, multiply 4 × 9 = 36, then add 3. The answer is 39.

Geometry formulas

Memorize these essentials: rectangle area = length × width; triangle area = ½ × base × perpendicular height; circle area = πr²; rectangular-prism volume = length × width × height.

Worked example: A triangle has a base of 12 inches and perpendicular height of 7 inches. Its area is ½ × 12 × 7 = 42 square inches. Area uses square units, whereas length and perimeter use linear units.

Exponents, roots and estimation

Remember that 4² means 4 × 4 = 16, not 4 × 2. Likewise √81 = 9 because 9² = 81. Estimate before choosing a multiple-choice answer: √50 lies between 7 and 8. Use factoring and number sense to eliminate impossible choices.
