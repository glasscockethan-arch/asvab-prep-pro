Our purpose

Better preparation, one question at a time.

Aptitude Beacon is an independent study website that provides practice questions, worked explanations, and subject-based review for learners preparing for the Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery.

What the platform offers

The practice app includes 540 educational questions covering nine subject areas, a diagnostic, timed practice modes, study notes, and locally stored results. We aim to improve clarity, variety, and accessibility with ongoing revisions.

What it doesn't do

It does not administer official tests, guarantee enlistment, calculate an official AFQT percentile, or determine military job eligibility. This site is not affiliated with the Department of Defense or any branch of the U.S. military.
