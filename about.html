Aptitude Beacon · About

Independent learning, with clear limits.

Aptitude Beacon offers six original introductory units in algebra, reading, statistics, research literacy, trade measurement and electrical theory. Lessons include objectives, worked examples and practice explanations. They are short introductions, not complete school courses.

Practice with a purpose

Use the learning library to explore a topic and My learning to track browser-local progress. The original ASVAB V3 app remains available with 540 questions and its existing progress format.

Honest educational scope

Content has author review, with independent educator review pending. Broader courses and credential exam preparation are in development. Practice percentages are not official AFQT percentiles or predictions of eligibility.

Independent by design

We are not affiliated with schools, colleges, the military, testing agencies, OSHA, EPA, NCCER or ASE. We do not issue credit, licenses, certifications or training completion cards.
