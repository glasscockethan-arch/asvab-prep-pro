Reports of unclear explanations, incorrect answers, broken features and accessibility barriers help improve the site.

Report a public technical or content issue

Use the project’s GitHub Issues page. Include the unit or question ID, the behavior you expected and a short description. A GitHub account may be required.

Issues are public. Do not include names, age, school details, personal contact information, identification, passwords, private scores or backup files. Young learners should ask a trusted adult for help with public reporting.

Private support is not available yet

The owner has not supplied a verified private support address. Do not send sensitive requests through a public issue. Establishing a private channel and reviewing the privacy policy are blockers to broad public launch.
