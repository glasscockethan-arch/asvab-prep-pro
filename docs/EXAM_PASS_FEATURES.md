## What the current personalization means

Recommendations use only this account's explicitly uploaded/readable V3 cloud history and completed guided sessions. Unsaved browser history does not upload. For each question, the latest response in the last 30 days wins; repeated/imported copies do not inflate distinct-question coverage. Correctness is derived from the existing canonical practice key, not imported `correct`/`answer` flags. Unknown, future/out-of-window and non-AFQT records are excluded transparently. Guided completed-result snapshots retain their bank version; the dashboard recomputes evidence with current keys.

Fewer than ten distinct answered items in an area is labeled baseline needed, not weakness. The 70% and 85% practice bands organize next steps; they have no validated official interpretation. Unanswered items are disclosed separately in the dashboard and count as incorrect in session scores. A finite/template-heavy pool and editable imports can limit interpretability even when software recalculates keys. Qualified review of all keys, explanations and these guidance rules remains required.

The saved calendar uses the priorities available when created. Later sessions prioritize known missed questions, then unseen items, then earlier practiced items within the assigned subject. The learner may explicitly replace the calendar as priorities change; there is no silent schedule reshuffle or attempt deletion. Prior plans/results remain in a private archive. Checkpoints cover AR, MK, WK and PC, not an official exam blueprint/length. Workload estimates are suggestions, not measured study time or a promise of sufficient preparation.

A 30/60/90-day calendar is distinct from the 90-day access entitlement. A later start/replacement does not extend trial or pass access. The dashboard displays access expiry; calendars extending beyond it can be exported and followed with free practice. No active-pass repurchase or automatic renewal is implemented.

## Honest launch promise and validation still needed

The value proposition is **organized practice and a clear next action**, plus private continuity. It does not sell a larger bank, official alignment, expert approval, coaching from a human tutor, adaptive psychometrics or a score guarantee. Keep the full free question bank, explanations, diagnostics/timers, recommendations, local history and backups accessible.

Before sale, the owner must approve this feature/value promise, independent educators must review the content/guidance, and beta testers must assess usability and whether the structure is useful enough to pay for. No willingness-to-pay or score-improvement evidence has been collected. Do not present successful automated scoring as that evidence. The existing $9.99/month Beacon Plus idea and new catalog expansion remain deferred.
