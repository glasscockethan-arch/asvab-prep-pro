Aptitude Beacon · Privacy & data

Your learning stays in this browser.

Updated October 25, 2026. This describes the current static site. It is not an assurance of legal compliance.

What is stored

The new platform stores lessons marked read, saved units, practice answers and dates, goals, unfinished sessions and a copy of readable ASVAB history under the browser-storage key aptitude-beacon-v1. Your theme preference uses aptitude-beacon-theme. The original ASVAB app uses asvab-prep-pro-v3; the new platform reads that key without replacing it.

No account, name, email address, birth date, school record or military application record is requested. Scores and interests can still be private information. Avoid storing identifying information in backup files.

Export, import and deletion

My learning exports a JSON backup, merges validated backups and deletes new-platform data after confirmation. Importing an ASVAB backup into the new platform does not change the original app. To restore the original app itself, use its Backup / Restore screen; that screen replaces its progress after confirmation.

Deleting new-platform progress leaves original ASVAB progress intact. The original app has its own confirmed deletion control. Clearing all browser data for this site deletes both, along with theme preferences. Export first. Downloads remain wherever you save them and must be deleted separately.

Local does not mean permanent or synchronized

Browser storage can be cleared, blocked or evicted. Private browsing and storage limits can affect retention. Backups are your responsibility. There is no automatic cross-device synchronization.

Hosting and network requests

GitHub Pages serves static HTML, scripts, styles and lesson data. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser details and request logs to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External reference links contact their destinations when you follow them.

No advertising or analytics scripts

The application adds no advertising cookies, behavioral tracking, analytics, accounts or third-party fonts. Future data collection requires a separate review and policy update before activation.

Young learners and support

Do not post names, age, school details, private scores or contact information to public issue reports. A private support method has not yet been established. See contact limitations. The owner must review applicable obligations for minors and provide a suitable private support channel before a broad launch.
