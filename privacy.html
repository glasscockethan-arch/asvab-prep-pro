Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Progress data

The practice application uses browser storage on your device to remember study progress and test results. We do not currently operate an account database or sync individual scores to a server. You can remove this data by clearing browser website data. Exported backup files remain wherever you choose to save or share them.

Website hosting

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other web hosts, the hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser details, and request logs in order to deliver the site and maintain security. Review GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Advertising

There are no advertising scripts installed on this website at this time. If we introduce advertising or analytics, we will update this policy to identify relevant providers, cookies, data use, and applicable consent choices before enabling them.

Contact

For privacy questions, see our contact page. We do not ask users to submit medical, financial, or military application records through the practice app.

Note: This policy should be reviewed and finalized with the site owner's verified contact information before applying for advertising.
