Aptitude Beacon · Privacy & data

Understand where your learning data lives.

Updated October 26, 2026. This distinguishes the browser-local learning site from the optional connected development beta. Owner, contact and jurisdiction-specific legal review remains required before public recruitment. It is not an assurance of legal compliance.

What is stored

The new platform stores lessons marked read, saved units, local course enrollments, practice answers and dates, chapter/final assessment results, goals, unfinished sessions and a copy of readable ASVAB history under the browser-storage key aptitude-beacon-v1. Your theme preference uses aptitude-beacon-theme. Unfinished ASVAB V3 practice uses a separate aptitude-beacon-asvab-session-v1 recovery record, including question IDs, choices, position and the original timer deadline. It remains on the device and is not part of cloud copies. The original ASVAB app uses asvab-prep-pro-v3; the new platform reads that key without replacing it.

The free learning app requests no account, name, email address, birth date, school record or military application record. Optional beta recruitment has separate data collection below. Scores and interests can still be private information. Avoid storing identifying information in backup files.

Export, import and deletion

My learning exports a JSON backup, merges validated backups and deletes new-platform data after confirmation. Importing an ASVAB backup into the new platform does not change the original app. To restore the original app itself, use its Backup / Restore screen; that screen replaces its progress after confirmation.

Deleting new-platform progress leaves original ASVAB progress intact. The original app has its own confirmed deletion control. Clearing all browser data for this site deletes both, along with theme preferences. Export first. Downloads remain wherever you save them and must be deleted separately.

Local does not mean permanent or synchronized

Browser storage can be cleared, blocked or evicted. Private browsing and storage limits can affect retention. Backups are your responsibility. There is no automatic cross-device synchronization. An invited tester can explicitly upload and merge a private cloud copy through a connected development beta account; see below.

Hosting and network requests

GitHub Pages serves static HTML, scripts, styles and lesson data. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser details and request logs to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External reference links contact their destinations when you follow them.

No advertising or third-party analytics scripts

The learning application adds no advertising cookies, behavioral tracking, third-party analytics or third-party fonts. The development beta uses an essential sign-in session cookie and private aggregate operational counters. Future data collection requires a separate review and policy update before activation.

Young learners and support

Do not post names, age, school details, private scores or contact information to public issue reports. A private support method has not yet been established. See contact limitations. The owner must review applicable obligations for minors and provide a suitable private support channel before a broad launch.

Optional free beta data

Consent version: beta-2026-10-26. Beta access is free and requires no card. Recruitment is initially limited to adults 18 or older. We ask only for an email address and, optionally, an expected ASVAB test date. Your affirmative consent permits storage and contact about beta access and beta feedback. It does not authorize marketing. No automatic email campaign or sale of contact information is implemented.

The form checks whether a private service is connected. On an unconnected static preview it is disabled, and no request is saved. A connected development service stores requests privately in a local database outside the website files. Signup information is not available in a public directory. The operator can review and export it with a local administrative command. Email addresses are normalized to lowercase to prevent basic duplicates; resubmitting does not edit an existing request.

Uninvited requests older than 90 days and feedback older than 90 days are removed when the service starts and during the documented owner cleanup. Database backups and CSV exports are separate private copies; the owner must delete them under an approved retention schedule. This development implementation restricts file permissions but does not encrypt the database or backups. Public hosting, encrypted storage, a verified private contact, deletion operations and the operator identity must be approved before real recruitment.

Invited beta accounts and cloud copies

The owner manually supplies a single-use, 30-minute sign-in link only to an opted-in address through a verified private channel. The service does not itself verify email delivery or send messages. A bearer session cookie lasts up to seven days and is inaccessible to JavaScript. This implementation runs only on local development HTTP; production HTTPS and Secure cookies remain release work.

A cloud upload happens only when you request it. It contains completed lesson activity, saved units, enrollments, answers, dates, goals and readable ASVAB history, including question and explanation text already present in your backup. Unfinished sessions and timers remain local. Cloud retrieval remains available to the signed-in account after free beta or test pass access expires. Local learning does not require an account.

Private feedback is stored for operator review with the signed-in account, category, optional question ID, message and date. Do not include sensitive information. You can delete your active development account, cloud copy, request and feedback from your beta account. Deletion does not erase browser progress or previously exported or backed-up copies. Account records are kept during the beta until you delete them or the operator closes the beta; a final retention and backup-deletion schedule is required before public use.

Payment tests and aggregate counters

The Exam Pass is planned at $19.99 one time for 90 days and is not on sale. No live payments are enabled. Specifically invited payment testers may be redirected to Stripe test Checkout; do not use a real card. The application does not collect or store card numbers. Its private database stores test checkout/payment identifiers, dates, entitlement and refund state. Stripe processes test-session data under Stripe’s privacy policy.

Aggregate counters record submitted beta requests, invitation redemptions, test checkouts, verified test payments, and test refunds/failures. There are no page-view beacons, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics scripts, or automatic marketing messages. Short-lived in-memory request throttles use the connection address; this app does not persist IP addresses or log form contents. Future hosting logs need a separate review.

Consent withdrawal and private support

A verified private owner/support channel has not yet been supplied. Do not post your email, invitation link or account details in a public GitHub issue. Invited testers can use the account deletion control; operators can privately delete an uninvited request after verifying the request. Establishing a private withdrawal/support route and identifying the data controller are blockers to public recruitment. Do not collect real beta signups until those are resolved.
