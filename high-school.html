Algebra: solving linear equations

To isolate a variable, undo operations in reverse order while keeping both sides balanced. In 3x + 7 = 25, subtract 7 from each side to get 3x = 18, then divide by 3: x = 6. Substitute 6 back into the equation to verify your result.

Reading: main idea and evidence

The main idea is the central claim or message a passage communicates. Supporting details provide examples, reasons, or evidence. When a multiple-choice answer sounds reasonable but is not supported by the passage, do not select it.

Science: independent and dependent variables

The independent variable is what an experimenter deliberately changes. The dependent variable is the observed response. If students grow seeds under different light conditions and measure height, light exposure is independent and plant height is dependent. Control other conditions when practical.
